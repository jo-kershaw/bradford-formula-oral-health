
# External reference: OHID hospital tooth extractions, Bradford and England

**Type:** Published external reference data.

**Underlying official source:** Office for Health Improvement and Disparities (OHID), *Hospital tooth extractions in 0 to 19 year olds 2025*, published 17 February 2026. Official publication: https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/hospital-tooth-extractions-in-0-to-19-year-olds-2025

**Machine-readable data:** *Hospital tooth extractions in 0 to 19 year olds 2018 to 2025 (machine-readable data file)*, linked from that publication (``Tidy_data_tooth_extractions_2018_to_2025.csv``). Bradford local-authority code: **E08000032**. England is the national comparison.

**Data provenance:** `ohid_bradford_england_all_index.csv` reproduces values from “Bradford versus national hospital dental activity (23 October 2026)”, subsection “Findings: all hospital extractions” (PDF page 117).

## Definitions

- All hospital tooth-extraction episodes (both caries and non-caries) in ages **0–19**.
- Geography: Bradford local authority versus England. Both series use OHID's HES-based episode definition.
- `financial_year_ending=2021` means **April 2020–March 2021**.
- Each series is indexed independently: **mean activity in financial years ending 2019 and 2020 = 100**.
- Indices are recorded to one decimal place. OHID rounds some local-authority published counts to the nearest five and suppresses small cells.
